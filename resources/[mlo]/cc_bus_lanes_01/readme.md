## **💻 About This Map**

Thank you for using our resource — we hope it improves realism on your server.

The **Legion Square Bus Lanes** add modern, realistic bus lanes to one of the busiest areas in Los Santos.
This map is fully integrated with GTA V’s existing traffic and NPC systems to ensure smooth and natural behavior.

### Location

* Legion Square: **(362.6381, -866.3199, 29.2232, 181.5821)**

The bus lanes are designed to blend seamlessly with the surrounding environment and maintain compatibility with default GTA mechanics.

## **📥 Installation**

1. Open your `resources` folder.

2. Drop the `cc_bus_lanes_01` folder into your desired location.

3. Add the following line to your `server.cfg`:

   ```cfg
   ensure cc_bus_lanes_01
   ```

4. Restart your server.

---

## **🎨 Customization**

This map uses **default GTA V road textures**.

* To change the appearance of the bus lanes, simply replace or reskin the original GTA V road textures.
* Because default textures are used, the bus lanes will **automatically adapt** to most vehicle and road reskin packs.
* No custom `.ytd` files are required for basic customization.

⚠️ **Note:** Editing GTA road textures will affect all roads using that texture across the map.
